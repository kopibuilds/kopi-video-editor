# Verify before delivery

Use the current brief and selected workflow/profile as the quality criteria.
Check a representative uncertain method before applying it to a batch. Document
evidence and limitations in the private project's VERIFY.md.

## Before rendering

- Inspect actual footage and source camera boundaries, not transcript text alone.
  Track framing to the source shot, including the source frames before its first
  camera cut. A fixed crop can lose a moving subject; inspect its full range.
- Preserve the approved caption master through visual revisions. Style, casing
  and timing changes are not permission to change its underlying words. Compare
  the delivered text with the approved master; retime it from the same EDL.
- Clean-read captions may omit stutters/fillers when authorized, preserving meaning.
  Do not invent uncertain names, words, connecting phrases or quotations. Record
  and resolve uncertainty rather than silently replacing it with a paraphrase.
- Identify speakers from audio or reliable labels checked against audio. Camera
  cuts, reaction shots, transcript text and loudness alone are not identity proof.
  Without verified turns, avoid speaker-specific gain or speaker colors. Neutral
  captions and unchanged source levels are reversible alternatives; explain limits.
- Review the requested cut boundaries with speech/context and natural tails.
  Waveforms, ASR and silence detection help locate candidates but do not establish
  that a word sounds complete. Never cut final consonants to hit an arbitrary length.

## Check the encoded file

- Extract **frame zero** and inspect **every frame of the first second**. A sample
  at 0.5 seconds is not first-frame verification. Check the requested thumbnail,
  both intended subjects, first caption/branding visibility and unwanted lead-in.
- Inspect transitions, editorial joins and layout changes with contiguous frame
  strips, plus caption extremes and moving faces at beginning/middle/end.
- Follow the selected layout: captions may need to move at a camera/layout cut
  even when the same phrase spans it. Check faces, hands, text boundaries and
  important source/product details at phone viewing size.
- Decode the whole delivered file; verify dimensions, duration, frame count,
  audio/video start times and joins. Check loudness/peaks when processing audio.
  If a visual-only revision promises unchanged audio, stream-copy and compare
  compressed audio hashes where practical.
- Listen to the opening, speech joins and ending when an audio-capable surface is
  available. Do not claim a listening review from screenshots, a waveform, ASR,
  a successful encode or a technical validator. State exactly what remains unverified.

Retain prior deliveries, update the timing map after changes, and identify the
latest reviewed output clearly. Checks supplement editorial judgment; neither
passing lint nor an isolated good frame proves the entire video is correct.
