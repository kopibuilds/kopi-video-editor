# Video to carousel, poster or Shorts

Read AGENTS.md, docs/ASSISTANT.md and docs/VIDEO-REPURPOSING.md. Use one source
workflow but keep each output's preferences separate. Produce only the requested
formats. Reuse docs/YOUTUBE.md and local transcription for source intake.

Check existing preferences first. When the requested output has no confirmed
setup, follow docs/REPURPOSE-SETUP.md: ask about optional branding, colours,
format and design; offer docs/REPURPOSE-STYLES.md or a supplied reference. Save
answers/assets in ignored local preferences/repurpose/, separated by output and
brand/project. Do not reset an existing profile or require setup of other outputs.

For Shorts, follow kopi-shorts and only its selected profile. For video carousels
and posters, load the relevant private static references and current brief, not
Shorts styling. Optional graphics templates apply only when explicitly selected.
Do not merge or overwrite saved design preferences.

Static outputs follow docs/IMAGE-EDITOR.md: editable HTML with PNG export by
default, flattering frame selection, accurate attribution and context. Moving
Shorts retain natural speech and pacing. Proposals follow docs/PROPOSALS.md.
